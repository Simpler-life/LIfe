# Curriculum Maintenance and Extension Rules

This curriculum is **versioned and adaptable**, not a frozen 48-week syllabus.

## When to add something
A new source/topic is justified if it is (a) a missing prerequisite, (b) a verified misunderstanding, (c) essential modern AI content, (d) a current course project, or (e) a motivated extension with clear outcome.

## Editing checklist
1. Identify track, prerequisite and learning objective. Check whether the topic already exists in Roadmap or another track.
2. Prefer **official/primary English resources** with deep links, plus optional accessible video; avoid dead links and dubious “best” claims.
3. Specify: **where to find it; exact section; what to do; independent deliverable; small checkpoint; pass criterion**.
4. Place the topic in existing current Week **only** if time fits 12–15h total. Otherwise put it in next Week or optional backlog.
5. Update the relevant Roadmap and [Study-System](../Learning-Plans/Study-System.md) if sequencing changes.
6. Update [STATE](STATE.md) and briefly document the change below; preserve original passed checkboxes and UDL agreement unless explicitly renegotiated.
7. Commit and verify GitHub.

## Course quality safeguards
- Course lecture videos may be restricted; fallback to legal publicly available notes/textbook.
- Avoid adding an entire course without explicit time rebalance.
- Prefer one meaningful independent task over five superficial readings.
- Pass threshold comes from [Session Protocol](SESSION-PROTOCOL.md), not from perceived motivation or number of hours.
