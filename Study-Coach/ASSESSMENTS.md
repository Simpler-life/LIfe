## Logging rules
- Status: PASS / RETRY / INCOMPLETE. Never infer a PASS from `- [x]` on reading alone.
- Short factual evidence only, e.g. “explained call vs return; traced conditionals; handled modified input”. Never store detailed personal responses or assignment solutions.
- For a retry: record target concept and next variant, not the answer key.
- The Week-level and CP milestone checks are **separate** ledger entries.
