## Conceptual sequence, not dated promises
1. Foundations: supervised/unsupervised/RL ideas, shallow and deep networks; interpret input, labels, architecture.
2. Optimization: losses, gradients, initialization, regularization and generalization; derive toy updates.
3. Modern architectures: convolution, residual networks, attention, transformers; draw tensor flow and causal masking.
4. Generative AI: latent variables, autoencoders, GANs, diffusion and training/sampling distinctions.
5. Practice: training loops, careful evaluation, ablations, representation, model limits and ethics.
